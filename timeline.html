Hansen Corporation Timeline

The 100 year long history

The start of Hansen Corporation

Hansen Corporation was founded in 1907 by Julius Hansen. Julius based the company in Princeton Indiana.

Julius was a German immigrant who started as a watchmaker and jeweler by trade.

As time went on Julius made at the request of a high school principle the first bell ringing system.

It didn't take long for Julius to expand the company and become a market leader in small motor manufacturing.

The motor products varied from DC brush motors, clock movements, hysteresis permanent magnet synchronous motors, and stepper motors.

Hansen Corporation remained independent from 1907 to 1977.

The disaster

On March 18, 1925 the historic Tri-state Tornado hit the company as the tornado's final destination in Princeton Indiana, completely destroying the companies production.

The tornado hit when the company was experiencing record high orders for the new Hansen Self-Winding Clock. This did not stop Julius Hansen as he and hisson, William Lester

worked on rebuilding the company which allowed the company to continue production and grow within the precision motor industry.

Mid 1900's

Hansen Corporation continued to grow and eventually expaned into synchronous motors, DC motors, clock movements, and custom motion products.

In 1977 the ownership changed to Minebea, a japanese manufacturer.
