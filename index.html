What do we do?

Hansen Corporation is a company known for making fractional horsepower electric motors, DC motors, gear motors, and motion control related motors. Hansen Corporation is a part of a larger organization known as Electrocraft.

This website provides a brief overview of the history of Hansen Corporation. Starting with the background, milestones, events, and contact information. Please visit the timeline section to learn more about Hansen Corporation and it's long 100 year long history.
