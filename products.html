Products

Hansen Corporation manufactures a variety of synchronous motors for industrial and commercial applications.

  • AC-600 series deep pear synchron K mount motor
  • AC-600 series pear synchron C mount motor
  • AC-600 series round synchron A & D mount motor

The typical applications that these motors are used for are:

  • Damper Controls
  • Clocks
  • Mechanicl Timers
  • Time Stamps
  • Chart Recorders
  • POP Displays

These motors are very diverse, some examples of their uses are:

  • Damper Controls handle HVAC heating and AC systems, they help open and close dampers to control the flow of air through the ducts.
  • Clocks can vary from analog wall locks, tower clocks, industrial clocks. This helps rotate the hands of a clock to help keep accurate times.
  • Valve Controls, these are used in water treatment plants, irrigation systems, or chemical processing. The motor helps close and open the valves at controlled speeds to regulate flows of liquids or gases.
  • Mechanical Timers are used in washing machines, ovens, and industrial machinery. These motors turn the internal gears to count down time and activate functions at the correct moment.
  • Time Stamps allow for employees to punch in for work or document information in real time. The motor rotates inside the machine to print out the current date and time on documents.
  • Chart Records use the motors to help monitor pressure equipment and temperatures. Slowly moves the chart paper ore cording pen so measurements are recorded accurately over time.
  • POP Displays are rotating product displays in retail stores. These slowly rotate or move a display to attract the attention of customers. (POP means Point of Purchase)

AC-600 Series deep pear synchron K mount

Synchron 600 series K mount is an AC timing motor that offers a deeper gearbox.

This means the revolutions can be slowed down to 1 revolution per week.

AC 600 series pear synchron C mount motor

The AC 600 series pear synchron C mount motor can rotate at speeds of 0.50 revolutions per hour to 360 revolutions per minute

This motor has a one way clutch and a two way clutch. A one way clutch allows the motor to drive a mechanism in one direction while allowing it to be manually reset without damaging the motor

Using the two way clutch allows extra protection from excessive loads with output speeds below 6 rpm.

Both clutches allow improved reliability and longer lifespans of the motor.

AC-600 series round synchron A & D mount motor

The AC-600 series round synchron A & D mount motor allows for you to change the speed of the motor from 0.8 RPM to 360 RPM.

This particular motor has 2 models, one that is a two-hole model which is the Model-D motor or the four-hold motor called the Model-A.
