Hansen Corporation manufactures a variety of synchronous motors for industrial and commercial applications.

Here are some of the motors that Hansen Corporation is currently producing.

  • Fractional Horsepower Motors
  • DC Motors
  • Gear Motors
  • Motion Control Motors

AC-600 Series deep pear synchron K mount

Synchron 600 series K mount is an AC timing motor that offers a deeper gearbox.

This means the revolutions can be slowed down to 1 revolution per week.

AC 600 series pear synchron C mount motor

The AC 600 series pear synchron C mount motor can rotate at speeds of 0.50 revolutions per hour to 360 revolutions per minute

This motor has a one way clutch and a two way clutch. A one way clutch allows the motor to drive a mechanism in one direction while allowing it to be manually reset without damaging the motor

Using the two way clutch allows extra protection from excessive loads with output speeds below 6 rpm.

Both clutches allow improved reliability and longer lifespans of the motor.
